Thanks for wanting to push this further!

This project is licensed under the **GNU AGPL-3.0** (see `LICENSE`). The maintainer also uses these packages inside a separate, private hosted product.

To make that possible, **by submitting a pull request you agree that:**

1. you wrote the contribution (or have the right to submit it), and
2. you license it to the project under AGPL-3.0, **and** you grant the maintainer a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free right to also use, modify and sublicense your contribution under other terms, including in closed-source products.

You keep the copyright in your work. A formal CLA bot will be added before the first outside pull request is merged; until then, please say "I agree to the contribution terms in CONTRIBUTING.md" in your PR description.

*This is a plain-language summary, not legal advice. The maintainer should have a lawyer review the final CLA text.*

## Rules for contributions

- Nothing here may mention or depend on payments, subscriptions or accounts. Authentication is a pluggable hook.
- Third-party assets (models, textures, maps, fonts, icons) need a licence that allows redistribution. Add each one to `ASSETS.md` with its source and licence.
- No secrets, analytics or tracking code.
